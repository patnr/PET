# MiniRes benchmark: results

Generated by `run.py`; do not edit by hand. See `cases.py` for the cases, `methods.py` for the
methods and their settings, and `scores.py` for what is scored.

- Each cell is the mean over the twin experiments (truths).
- The ± is the standard error of the cell's *difference* from the reference row's (ES).
  Every row is run on the same truths, from the same prior ensembles,
  so that variability due to these somewhat cancels. `wins vs ES` counts the truths on which
  the row's forecast CRPS beats ES's: two rows other than ES are told apart only roughly, by their ±.
- ⚠: worse than the prior, by more than twice the standard error of the difference.
- The misfit is the mean squared normalized residual, over members and observations: about 2 at
  the noise level, 1 for the truth. Being heavy-tailed, it is shown as the median over the truths,
  with the number of truths on which the posterior fits worse than the prior did. A low misfit is
  not necessarily good; could be overfitting.
- `spread/RMSE` is about 1 for a calibrated ensemble; well below it, overconfident.
- Failed simulations are left out of the data and forecast scores, and counted in `failed members`,
  a column shown only when there were any.
- A case other than `base` shows only the table that carries its story; the others are summed
  up in a line, and `python run.py --show` prints every table of every case.

## Case `base`: 50 x 50 cells

The base case.

20 truths, 50 members; history of 16 of 40 steps, observed at steps [2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16]. PET at `4fca540`, 2026-11-03.

**Findings.** The data are worth having: the field's RMSE falls by 43 % (0.80 to 0.45, margIS tuned; 0.47 for LM-EnRML and distance localization) and the forecast's CRPS by 75 % (0.142 to 0.035, distance localization), and the field and the forecast rank the rows alike. Iterating beats members: LM-EnRML at 50 members (0.046) beats ES at 125 (0.057) for the same 250 simulations, on 15 of 20 truths. Localization beats both: distance localization at the prior's range (0.035) beats LM-EnRML by 0.011 ± 0.004 (14/20) and the best unlocalized row, margIS tuned, by 0.018 ± 0.005 (18/20), at the same field RMSE and better calibrated (spread/RMSE 0.73 against 0.46). ES-MDA trails LM-EnRML (0.056 against 0.046, 13/20 to LM) and is the most overconfident row (spread/RMSE 0.30). GN-EnRML at PET's default step lengths is no better than ES-MDA; at γ max = 1 (tuned) it forecasts like LM-EnRML but collapses more (0.30). EnIF stays calibrated (1.0) but learns the field less (RMSE 0.54) and forecasts no better than ES with 125 members. Adaptive localization at the universal or 3σ threshold is likewise no better than ES at 125 members; at 1.5σ (tuned) it matches LM-EnRML, but is far behind distance localization (0.050 against 0.035, 15/20 to distance) and learns less of the field (0.56 against 0.47). For PET: GN-EnRML's default γ max = 0.5 and the adaptive localization's default thresholds are poor on this case; the distance localization's and LM-EnRML's defaults stand.

## Case `coarse`: 25 x 25 cells

The base case at 25 x 25 cells: a fifth of the cost, for quick checks. Its smeared fronts make the history less nonlinear, and the methods harder to tell apart.

20 truths, 50 members; history of 16 of 40 steps, observed at steps [2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16]. PET at `4fca540`, 2026-11-03.

**Findings.** The picture of `base` at a fifth of the cost: the field improves by 44 %, distance localization forecasts best (0.028), ES-MDA is the most overconfident row (0.29). The differences between the iterating rows are smaller: ES-MDA is within 0.006 ± 0.003 of LM-EnRML, and GN-EnRML tuned within 0.002. EnIF does much better here (0.048, against 0.071 on `base`): its neighbourhood is counted in cells, so it reaches further on a coarse grid. Good enough for a quick check; not for ranking the iterating schemes.

## Case `rough`: 50 x 50 cells

A correlation range of 300 m instead of 450: more degrees of freedom in the field than the data can fix, so spurious correlations cost more. Where ES-MDA at 50 members collapses and localization should pay most.

20 truths, 50 members; history of 16 of 40 steps, observed at steps [2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16]. PET at `4fca540`, 2026-11-03.

**Findings.** Where localization should pay, it does, on the field: with a range of 300 m the data improve the field by only 22 % at best (distance localization, 0.62; EnIF, 0.63), and ES, ES-MDA and GN-EnRML tuned end up worse than the prior on it (⚠), every unlocalized iterating row overconfident (spread/RMSE 0.26 to 0.60). Distance localization's field RMSE beats LM-EnRML's by 0.067 ± 0.017 (18/20). On the forecast the ordering is as on `base` (distance localization 0.039, LM-EnRML 0.046, ES-MDA 0.054), the margins a little narrower. The adaptive localization at 1.5σ matches LM-EnRML on the forecast but not on the field (0.74, worse than EnIF's 0.63 and the distance localization's 0.62).

The table of the forecast is omitted: the picture is that of `base`, as the findings say. Every score is in `results/rough-ne50.json`.

## Case `viscous`: 50 x 50 cells

Oil 20 times as viscous as water: the water breaks through early and abruptly, so the history is strongly nonlinear in the permeability. Where iterating should pay most.

20 truths, 50 members; history of 16 of 40 steps, observed at steps [2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16]. PET at `4fca540`, 2026-11-03.

**Findings.** Where iterating should pay, it pays most: ES at 0.068 against LM-EnRML's 0.030, ES-MDA's 0.036, and distance localization's 0.023 (20/20). ES at 125 members (0.040) is beaten by LM-EnRML at 50 on 17 of 20 truths. The step control matters more here than elsewhere: GN-EnRML at its default step lengths (0.048) is the worst of the iterating rows, and its tuned γ max = 1 (0.036) is worse than LM-EnRML by 0.007 ± 0.002 (18/20 to LM). ES-MDA's four steps at α = 4 lose less to LM-EnRML here (0.006 ± 0.004) than its collapse (spread/RMSE 0.25) would suggest. On the field, the picture is that of `base`, a little better for every row (LM-EnRML 0.44, distance localization 0.43, ES-MDA 0.54 at spread/RMSE 0.25).

## Case `scalars`: 50 x 50 cells

The base case with the residual oil saturation `sor` unknown too (prior 0.22 ± 0.04): a global scalar next to the field, which a localization must leave alone, and which the water cuts constrain only late.

20 truths, 50 members; history of 16 of 40 steps, observed at steps [2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16]. PET at `4fca540`, 2026-11-03.

**Findings.** The unknown `sor` (prior error 0.032, sd 0.040) is learnt by the unlocalized iterating rows to an error of about 0.020, with a spread of 0.008 to 0.015: overconfident by two to three times. ES learns nothing of it (0.029) but shrinks its spread to 0.014 all the same. EnIF and the adaptive localization at the universal threshold leave it at the prior: they localize a global scalar away. The distance localization leaves `sor` unlocalized, as `run.py` tells it to, and does as well as LM-EnRML on it (0.022). The extra unknown costs ES-MDA on the forecast (0.060, against 0.056 on `base`, and now 8/20 against LM-EnRML) and LM-EnRML nothing (0.047); otherwise the forecast and the field show the picture of `base`.

The tables of the forecast and the field are omitted: the picture is that of `base`, as the findings say. Every score is in `results/scalars-ne50.json`.
