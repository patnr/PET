# MiniRes benchmark: results

Generated by `run.py`; do not edit by hand. See `cases.py` for the cases, `methods.py` for the
methods and their settings, and `scores.py` for what is scored.

- Each cell is the mean over the twin experiments (truths).
- The ± is the standard error of the cell's *difference* from the reference row's (ES).
  Every row is run on the same truths, from the same prior ensembles,
  so that variability due to these somewhat cancels.
- ⚠: worse than the prior, by more than twice the standard error of the difference.
- A lower (data) misfit is not necessarily better; could be overfitting.
- Failed simulations are left out of the data and forecast scores, and counted in `failed members`.

## Case `coarse`: 25 x 25 cells

The base case.

20 truths, 50 members; history of 16 of 40 steps, observed at steps [4, 8, 12, 16]. PET at `4fca540`, 2026-10-30.

**Findings.** ES-MDA and LM-EnRML fit the data to about the noise level (misfit about 2) and forecast best of the defaults (CRPS 0.044 and 0.049, against ES's 0.063); GN-EnRML at its default step lengths barely improves on ES. The localizations and EnIF fit the data less closely, but keep more spread and score best on the field (logK CRPS about 0.405). Tuning helps the localizations most: distance localization at 600 m forecasts as well as ES-MDA, and scores better on the field.
